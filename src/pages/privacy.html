Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Panawala CPA LLC ("Panawala, CPA", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website, send us a message or book a consultation.

What information we collect

Information you give us. When you use the consultation questions or contact form, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The type of client you are (for example, an individual or a business owner), your industry, business structure and approximate business size
  • The services you are looking for, your main tax, accounting or business concerns, and your goals
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in a message or the optional notes field

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view.

Please do not send sensitive information through this website. Do not include Social Security numbers, tax documents, bank account details or other sensitive financial information in the consultation questions or contact form. If we need that information, we will ask for it through a secure method after you become a client.

How we use it

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your messages and confirm your booking
  • Read your answers before we meet so we can prepare for your consultation
  • Provide, manage and improve our services and this website
  • Send you service-related communications
  • Meet our legal and professional obligations

We do not use your information for purposes that are unrelated to these.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting, scheduling and calendar, email, and video meeting tools, which may use it only to provide their services to us
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it or to protect our legal rights
  • A successor, if our business is ever sold or reorganized, subject to this policy

Information you provide after you become a client is also handled under your engagement letter and the confidentiality rules that apply to CPAs.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work and to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you turn them off, some parts of the site may not work as expected.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet legal, accounting and professional record-keeping requirements. When we no longer need it, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No website or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or stop sending you messages, by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will respond to requests as those laws require.

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
